Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

1. Who we are

Walkart ("we", "us", "our") operates the affiliate marketing platform at walkart.us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Service.

2. Information we collect

From publishers and merchants: name, email address, website URL, payment details, and usage data collected during account registration and platform use.

From end users (visitors to publisher sites): our script collects anonymized click and transaction data to attribute commissions. We do not collect names, email addresses, or personal identifiers from end users without their consent.

Automatically: IP addresses, browser type, referrer URLs, and cookie identifiers used for attribution and fraud prevention.

3. How we use your information

  • To operate the affiliate tracking and commission payment system
  • To verify publisher eligibility and prevent fraud
  • To send account-related communications (payment notifications, policy updates)
  • To improve our platform and reporting tools
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies and tracking

Our affiliate tracking script sets a first-party cookie on publisher sites to attribute sales to the correct publisher. This cookie has a 30-day lifespan. We also use analytics cookies to understand platform usage. Publishers must disclose Walkart's tracking to their end users in their own privacy policies.

5. Data sharing

We share data with affiliate network partners solely to track and validate commissions. We do not sell personal data to third parties. We may share data with service providers (hosting, fraud detection, payment processors) under strict data processing agreements.

6. Data retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active plus 3 years for accounting purposes. Transaction logs are retained for 7 years for audit compliance. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. To exercise these rights contact legal@walkart.us.

8. Security

We use TLS encryption for all data in transit and AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.

9. Children

Our Service is not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email to registered users at least 14 days before taking effect.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: legal@walkart.us — or visit our contact page.
