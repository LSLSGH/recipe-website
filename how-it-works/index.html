How Walkart works

Automatic affiliate monetization in minutes — for any type of publisher, any type of content.

For publishers

From signup to first commission in 5 minutes

1

Create your free publisher account

Sign up at walkart.us/publishers with your website URL and publisher type. Approval typically takes under 24 hours. There is no minimum traffic requirement and no fees to join.

Create account →
2

Add one line of code to your site

Copy your personalized Walkart snippet from your dashboard and paste it just before the closing </body> tag on every page of your site. That's all the technical setup you'll ever need.

<script type="text/javascript" src="https://s.skimresources.com/js/304338XXXXXXXX.skimlinks.js"></script>
3

Links are converted automatically

Our script scans every page on load. When it detects a link to one of our 48,500+ partner merchants, it automatically converts it into a tracked affiliate link. No manual work. No link replacement. It works on existing content too.

4

Readers click, buy, and you earn

When a visitor clicks a converted link and makes a purchase, you earn a commission. Rates vary by merchant — typically 2%–20% of the sale value. All earnings are tracked in real time in your dashboard.

5

Get paid monthly

Commissions are consolidated across all networks and paid in a single monthly payment via bank transfer or PayPal. Minimum payout threshold is $10. No chasing multiple networks for separate payments.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Walkart really free to join? +
Yes — there are no setup fees, monthly fees, or minimum traffic requirements for publishers. Walkart earns a small percentage of the commissions we generate for you, so our incentives are fully aligned.
Will the script slow down my website? +
No. The Walkart script loads asynchronously, meaning it does not block your page from rendering. It adds less than 15ms to page load time in typical conditions and has no impact on Core Web Vitals.
Which merchants are in the network? +
48,500+ merchants across fashion, electronics, travel, beauty, home, sports, food, and more — in over 40 countries. The network includes Amazon, ASOS, Nike, Apple, Booking.com, and thousands of independent brands.
Do I need to apply to each merchant individually? +
No. A single Walkart account gives you access to all 48,500+ merchants simultaneously. You do not need to apply to individual affiliate programs.
How long does approval take? +
Most publisher applications are reviewed within 24 hours. Our team manually checks each submission to ensure quality. You will receive an email confirmation once approved.
What is the minimum payout? +
The minimum payout threshold is $10. Payments are processed monthly via bank transfer or PayPal. Enterprise publishers on custom plans can negotiate weekly or real-time payouts.
Does Walkart work with AMP pages? +
Yes. For AMP pages, use the amp-skimlinks component: <amp-skimlinks layout="nodisplay" publisher-code="YOUR_CODE"></amp-skimlinks> inside the body of your AMP page, and add the extension script in the head.
